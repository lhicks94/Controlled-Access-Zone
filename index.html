Controlled Access Zone

Call tree and overhead entry permit process

Before Entering a Controlled Access Zone

Entry into a Controlled Access Zone is prohibited until authorization is received and the permit process has been completed.

Required Entry Process

  1. Contact personnel in the order listed below.
  2. If the first person does not answer, proceed to the next person.
  3. Obtain authorization from one of the representatives listed in the call tree.
  4. Complete the required Overhead Protection Entry Permit process.
  5. Enter the area only after authorization and permit requirements have been completed.

After receiving authorization from one of the representatives listed above, complete the Overhead Protection Entry Permit before entering the controlled access zone.

Overhead Entry Permit Process:
  1. Select Start Overhead Entry Permit below.
  2. The JE Dunn Safety Checklist Hub will open.
  3. Select Overhead Protection Entry Permit from the checklist library.
  4. Complete and submit the permit before entering the controlled access zone.
